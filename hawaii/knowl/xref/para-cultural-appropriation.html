On Cultural Appropriation.

When working with cultures distinct from our own, it is fundamental to approach the culture with an awareness of its purpose. We must ask ourselves: why are we utilizing this information? What are our goals with this lesson, and how does the inclusion of this culture enrich those outcomes?
🔗
This activity should be approached with the goal of students learning about and appreciating a culture different from theirs. We encourage you to have conversations with students about different ways of life and perspectives of the world.
🔗
The conversations around environmentalism in this lesson are especially important as they highlight a critical issue in the Hawaiian community: respecting the natural environment and allowing natives to have autonomy over the land. Students must understand the context of how native Hawaiians interacted with their world and the significance of their beliefs about preservation and respect.
🔗
Certain aspects of Hawaiian culture can be difficult to research, as much knowledge has been lost over time. We sought sources of information that come directly from native peoples and that accurately reflect their knowledge. We encourage you to do further research about the native Hawaiian peoples and inform yourself about the issues occurring in their communities.
